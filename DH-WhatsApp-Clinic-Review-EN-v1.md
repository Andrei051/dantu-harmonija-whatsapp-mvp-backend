## 1. What the assistant can and cannot do

**The assistant can:**

- answer general questions about the clinic;
- provide information about clinic services;
- provide confirmed / published prices;
- provide address, opening hours and contacts;
- explain basic first-visit information;
- reply in Lithuanian or English.

**The assistant cannot:**

- diagnose or assess a patient’s health condition;
- give medical or treatment advice;
- choose or recommend a treatment;
- book, change or cancel appointments;
- see or provide available appointment times;
- provide information the clinic has not confirmed.

- Phone: +370 610 11 222 / (8-5) 27 222 11

The assistant *can* currently say:

> “Thanks for your message. A team member will review it and get back to you soon.”

**Will someone at the clinic regularly review WhatsApp messages received on this number and be able to reply?**
