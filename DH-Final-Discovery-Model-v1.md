## Methodological lesson

**Discovery, demand, visibility, and trust are separate layers.**  
They interact. They must not be treated as the same thing.

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| Demand | What patients want |
| Visibility | What Google exposes |
| Discovery | How patients find DH |
| Trust | Why they choose DH |

**`F-EXP002-001`:** Strong local demand ≠ strong clinic SERP visibility for that category.

## Final Discovery Model v1

### Layer 1 — Trust

**Brand**  
Primary trust and conversion layer.  
Supported throughout GSC (brand dominates clicks), GBP (brand/location/reviews share), and live SERPs (generic clinic/dentist are weak acquisition surfaces).

### Layer 2 — People

**Named clinicians**  
Independent discovery pathway (`F-CAND005-001`).  
Generic `odontologas vilnius` ≠ DH strength. Named clinician queries → DH `/specialistai/` profiles ~1–2.

Two observed archetypes:
1. **DH-dominant** — e.g. Marius Bučinskas, Eugenija Bučinskienė  
2. **Multi-clinic market** — e.g. Vitalija Tučė, Deividas Blažys, Monika Ambrasienė, Vytautas Sabataitis  

Multi-clinic competition is a *stronger* reputation signal than pure DH exclusivity, not a weaker one.

### Layer 3 — Strong DH discovery surfaces

| Surface | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Orthodontics** | Strongest observed organic surface (`breketai` ~3, `dantų tiesinimas` ~1) |
| **Implants** | Strong organic (~3); weak local/sponsored — research-heavy discovery (H4 textbook) |
| **Whitening** | Efficient discovery surface inside a **saturated specialist market** (`F-EXP003-001`). GBP #1 clinical demand + GSC efficiency + crowded SERP are compatible |

### Layer 4 — Market demand surfaces

| Surface | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Prosthetics** | Strong demand (GBP duopoly); weak DH generic SERP visibility |
| **Esthetics** | Strong demand (GBP duopoly); weak DH generic SERP visibility |

Refined statement: these are **major demand engines**, not proven DH visibility engines on generic Vilnius SERPs.

### Layer 5 — Weak discovery surfaces

| Surface | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Generic clinic (`dantų klinika vilnius`) | Weak — homepage ~20+ |
| Generic dentist (`odontologas vilnius`) | Not meaningful — DH absent across 9 pages |
